SF health inspections: does enforcement work?

When a food facility fails an inspection or is closed, does it improve, and does the improvement hold?

Read the numbers with these caveats. One row per inspection event after latest-record dedup; farmers-market umbrella permits excluded from facility timelines. Inspection types are backfilled by DPH with roughly a year's lag, so recent "Reinspection" labels are incomplete; the funnel uses ratings, which do not lag. A failure with no later rated visit is censored, not resolved: registry cross-checks show at least 13 of 150 such facilities closed for good. Post-enforcement facilities fail their next check at roughly 3x the base rate; the data cannot say whether that is enforcement failing or selection of trouble-prone facilities.
